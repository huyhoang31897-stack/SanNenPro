SANNENPRO 2.0 - CONG CU TINH KHOI LUONG SAN NEN

CAI DAT
- Giai nen, giu nguyen thu muc SanNenPro. Khong can cai Python.
- Doc file DWG can may co AutoCAD. Khong co AutoCAD thi luu ban ve sang DXF.

CHUAN BI BAN VE
- Cao do tu nhien: TEXT so, MTEXT so, POINT co Z, hoac block co thuoc tinh cao do.
- Ranh gioi: polyline kin, moi khu 1 polyline.
- Cao do thiet ke: nhap tay, hoac TEXT so tren layer rieng.
- Duong gay (neu co): line/polyline tren layer co chu "break" hoac "duong gay".
  Line co Z thi lay Z do; line Z = 0 thi lay cao do tu mat dat.
- Ten layer tuy y: chuong trinh tu nhan. Neu nhan sai, bam "Doc danh sach layer" va chon lai.

SU DUNG
1. Mo SanNenPro.exe, bam Them de chon DWG/DXF.
2. Nhap cao do thiet ke, buoc luoi, he so K.
3. Can taluy thi nhap m cua mai dao, mai dap (vi du 1 va 1.5). De trong la khong tinh.
4. Bam CHAY. Xong bam Mo ket qua.

DUNG TRONG AUTOCAD
1. Mo SanNenPro.exe 1 lan.
2. Trong AutoCAD: APPLOAD, chon sannen.lsp (cho vao Startup Suite de tu nap).
3. Go SANNEN de mo chuong trinh voi ban ve hien hanh.
4. Tinh xong go SANNENKQ de chen ket qua dung toa do vao ban ve.

KET QUA
- SanNen_KhoiLuong.xlsx: tong hop, bang tung khu, can doi dat, can bang, taluy, kiem tra.
- SanNen_MatBang.dwg/.dxf: ranh gioi, luoi, cao do nut, hatch dao dap, duong 0, chan taluy, bang tong hop.
- SanNen_BaoCao.pdf: ban do dao dap, tong hop, duong cong can bang, canh bao.
File goc khong bi sua.

CACH TINH
- Luoi o: V = S x trung binh |h| cac dinh, tach theo duong h = 0.
- TIN: tich phan chinh xac tren mo hinh tam giac, co ep theo duong gay.
- Taluy: mai tu ranh gioi ra ngoai den khi gap mat dat, goc bo tron, tinh tren luoi 0.5 m, rong toi da 30 m.
- Nhieu khu chong len nhau: phan chong tinh cho khu nam truoc; khe ho nhap vao khu ke.

GIOI HAN
- Taluy ap dung cho toan bo chu vi ngoai, chua chon duoc tung canh.
- Chua tinh boc phong hoa, co ket, ham, ho mong.
- Can bang dao dap la cao do phang trong ranh, chua gom taluy.
- Xem sheet Kiem tra truoc khi dung so lieu.
